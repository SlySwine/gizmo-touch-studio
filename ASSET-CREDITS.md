- Gizmo GLB: optimized copy of Michael’s original revised Astra character, preserving native body/hat/eyes. Original Blender scenes and rendered outputs remain untouched.
- Neon Wilds architecture: original geometry generated in Blender 5.2.1 using `tools/build_neon_world.py`; no downloaded models or paid asset services.
- Dynamic mechanisms, character fur, atmosphere and game sounds: generated locally in Three.js and Web Audio.
- Three.js 0.180.0 and vendored examples: MIT license in `dist/vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt`.

No tracking, external CDN, account service or remote save backend.
